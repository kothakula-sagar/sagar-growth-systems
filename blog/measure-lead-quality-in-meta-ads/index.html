How to measure lead quality in Meta Ads

A low cost per lead only tells you what it cost to collect a contact. If you sell a service like road freight, you also need to know whether those contacts had a shipment you could handle and whether they booked.

Define a qualified lead before you launch

For a freight campaign, record whether the pickup and delivery locations are serviceable, whether the shipment fits your vehicle or service type, and when the customer needs pickup. Use the same criteria across campaigns so the comparison is meaningful.

Track what happens after the form

In your CRM or lead tracker, keep the campaign and ad names with each lead. Mark each lead as contacted, qualified, quoted, or booked as it progresses. Keep a reason for unqualified leads, such as a route you don’t serve or an unreachable number.

Compare campaigns by cost per qualified lead

Divide ad spend by qualified leads to find your cost per qualified lead. Divide spend by booked customers to find your cost per customer. Compare leads collected during the same period and allow enough time for the sales team to follow up before drawing conclusions.
