India PTL and FTL Freight Maps

Abra Freight Atlas is an interactive India freight map for shortlisting industrial and distribution locations. The india-ptl-ftl-maps project on GitHub includes 104 locations and lets you filter them by state and freight type.

What do PTL and FTL mean in freight?

PTL means part truckload. Several shipments can share the same truck. FTL means full truckload, where the shipper books the truck for a dedicated load. When planning coverage or advertising, start with the service your team can actually provide from each pickup location.

How to use the India freight map

  1. Search for a location or filter by state and freight type.
  2. Check each shortlisted location against your pickup coverage, vehicle availability, and lane economics.
  3. Review the suggested map pin and radius for Meta Ads. Adjust the radius to match the area you can serve.
  4. Export your shortlist as a CSV with latitude, longitude, and radius for campaign planning.

What the map priorities represent

The priority labels are editorial planning estimates. Confirm service coverage and freight demand with your operations data before using the shortlist to allocate ad spend.
